# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-05
First release. Seven skills for an entry-level job search, each complete in a plain chat with no tools:
cv-review, job-fit, cv-tailor, cover-letter, interview-prep, application-tracker, offer-compare.
An eval per skill graded three ways (invented facts, which counts double; wording; shape) by a strict judge: no invented fact in 21 of 21 runs, honest wording in 18 of 21, against 6 and 2 of 21 for Claude without the plugin. Tests that fail when a sentence carrying one of the plugin's promises is removed.
Reviewed before release, and corrected against what the strict judge quoted: the rule forbids upgrading what the user did, deriving new facts from old ones, inventing an outcome, a motivation, a learning plan, a lesson learned or any part of a STAR story, and labelling a skill on the user's behalf; the plugin's own commentary marks a guess as a guess; care with personal details and web search; no merged or percentage fit scores; pay rules for 13th salaries, hourly and net figures.
