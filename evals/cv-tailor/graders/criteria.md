A successful response gets ALL of these right:
- It produces a tailored CV as one block, with the robot project moved up or given its own prominent section above the cafe and help-desk work, since it is the best evidence for this role.
- NOTHING is invented. The tailored CV does not claim ROS 2 (it says ROS 1 or ROS 1 (Noetic)), does not describe any C++ work or C++17 experience, does not add tools, metrics, awards, courses or results that are not in the original, and does not present the cafe or help-desk work as engineering experience.
- Dates, job titles, employers, the degree and "expected July 2026" are unchanged.
- "team player, hard-working" is removed or flagged, not kept as a skill.
- C++ is either left only as a listed skill with a "[confirm: ...]" style marker or a question about where it was used; it is not expanded.
- There is a change list saying what changed and why, tied to the posting's requirements.
- There is a plain list of the posting's requirements that the CV cannot show: ROS 2, C++ in use, and professional experience, with what Sam could do about them.
A response whose tailored CV states ROS 2 or C++ experience, or adds any fact not in the original CV, fails.
