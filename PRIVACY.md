# Privacy

Career Desk is a set of instructions (skills) for Claude. This page says what that means for your data.

## What Career Desk collects

Nothing. The plugin contains no code that runs for you, no server and no tracking. Its author receives no data from your use of it: not your CV, not your job postings, not your conversations, not the fact that you used it.

## What it reads

When you use a skill, Claude reads what you put into the conversation: your CV, the postings you paste, the offers and applications you describe. A CV is personal data. Career Desk only tells Claude how to work with it.

## Where your data goes

- Your conversation, including anything you paste, is handled by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you are using. Career Desk does not change that and adds no other recipient.
- The skills instruct Claude never to put your CV text, your name or your contact details into a web search. If you ask it to look up an employer and web search is available, it searches with the company and role names only.
- In Claude Code or Cowork, the application tracker saves a file (`applications.md`) on your own machine, and only when you ask for a file. In the Claude apps it keeps nothing between conversations.

## What it will not ask for

Your age or date of birth, a photo, your marital or family status, your health, your religion, your nationality or ethnicity, your gender or sexual orientation, military or national service, immigration status, or a criminal record. It does not suggest adding them to a CV.

## What you can do

- Remove your phone number and home address before pasting a CV. No skill needs them.
- Leave referees' names and contact details off your CV; share them when an employer asks, with the referee's consent.
- Delete a conversation in Claude to remove it, under the terms of your Claude product.
